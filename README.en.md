# History of the War of Resistance Against Japan (OCR text)

*抗日戰史* (*History of the War of Resistance Against Japan*), 101 volumes, compiled by the Bureau of Historical and Political Compilation and Translation (史政編譯局) of the Ministry of National Defense, Republic of China, and published by the Bureau in May of the 55th year of the Republic (1966). The original is typeset vertically in Traditional Chinese.

This repository contains the **OCR text** of all 101 volumes, arranged by the chapter structure of the original tables of contents, plus colour scans of the pages that hold charts, maps and tables.

> [!WARNING]
> **The text is based on OCR, and most volumes have not been proofread character by character** (see "How it was made" for each volume's level of checking). Expect some wrong, missing or extra characters and punctuation errors. Check the original before quoting.

## Copyright status

This book was compiled by the Bureau of Historical and Political Compilation and Translation of the Ministry of National Defense, Republic of China, and published by the Bureau in May 1966. It is in the public domain in mainland China and Taiwan because its copyright term has expired.

Under Article 23(2) of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (Article 21(2) before the 2020 amendment; jurisdiction: mainland China, excluding Hong Kong and Macau) and Article 33 of the Copyright Act of the Republic of China (current jurisdiction: Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu), a work whose copyright is held by a legal entity enters the public domain 50 years after its first publication, or 50 years after its creation if it was not published within that time. Other works enter the public domain 50 years after the author's death.

The book was first published in 1966, so its protection ended on 31 December 2016.

## Contents

The work is arranged in parts:

- **Vol. 1**: general table of contents
- **Vols. 2–10**: the world situation before the war and the state of China and Japan; the causes of the war; a summary of the whole war
- **Vols. 11–88**: the individual operations and battles — from the Yuguan and Rehe operations, the January 28 Shanghai operations and the July 7 Incident with the Beiping–Tianjin operations, through the battles of Xuzhou, Shanghai, Taiyuan and Wuhan and the three battles of Changsha, to the battles of Central Henan, Changsha–Hengyang, Guilin–Liuzhou and West Hunan and the pursuit on the southern front
- **Vols. 89–98**: guerrilla warfare (Shandong–Jiangsu, Hebei–Chahar, Shanxi–Suiyuan and other regions)
- **Vols. 99–101**: acceptance of the surrender; demobilization

## File layout

One folder per volume, named "three-digit volume number + original title" (the part title as printed in the running head). The main files keep the name of the scanned source file. Volume 54, for example:

```
054 桂南會戰/
├── 54.md             structured text: chapter headings at the levels of the book's contents, chart pages embedded as images (Markdown)
├── 54.txt            the same text as plain text (UTF-8 with BOM)
├── 54.wiki           the same text as plain Wikitext, ready to paste into Wikisource
├── 待核清单.md        open items for the volume: untranscribed charts, doubtful characters, blank-page decisions
└── figures/          colour scans of the chart/table pages, with the "淞滬" watermark removed
    └── 54_p0012.jpg  p0012 is the page's position in the scan file, not the printed page number
```

Volume 5 also has a hand-proofread file, `05_已校對.md`.

## How it was made, and known gaps

- **Source**: DjVu scans (volume 89: a PDF scan)
- **Recognition**: OvisOCR2, run locally
- **Structure**: heading levels (part, chapter, section and the lower levels) follow each volume's own contents and the general contents in volume 1. Running heads (book title, part title, page number) and stray margin fragments are removed; sentences broken across pages were checked pair by pair and joined
- **Level of checking**:
  - Vol. 2: proofread by hand, page by page
  - Vols. 3–16 and 101: re-recognized in full with HunyuanOCR; the two texts were compared character by character and each difference decided
  - All other volumes: only the recurring misreadings confirmed in that comparison were corrected (e.g. 南濤路 → 南潯路); no character-by-character proofreading
  - All volumes: about 420 open items (doubtful text, sentences broken across pages, blank pages) were each checked against the page image; pages the original OCR missed were filled in from HunyuanOCR and checked against the image
- **Character forms**: the book's own glyphs (旣, 卽, 爲, 僞 …) are kept, and so are its misprints and inconsistent dates. However, OCR sometimes read variant forms such as 囘 and 欵 as the standard characters; these have not all been restored
- **Charts are not transcribed**: vertical tables, maps and organization charts are kept only as colour scans (3,450 images, watermark removed); the text inside them is not transcribed
- Each volume's remaining open items are listed in its `待核清单.md`

Totals: 101 volumes · 13,341 scanned pages · 3,450 chart scans · about 5.66 million characters (excluding whitespace)
